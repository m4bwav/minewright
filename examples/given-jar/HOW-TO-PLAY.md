# How to play given.jar

There is a big dark ball floating in your world. It has one eye. The eye is always watching.

## When the eye opens

- The eye wants **one thing**. You see a picture of it in the middle of your screen.
- Everybody wants the **same thing**.
- **Every player** must bring their **own one**.
- You have **30 minutes**. The clock is at the bottom of your screen.

## How to give it

1. Find or make the thing.
2. Walk up to the ball.
3. Look at it and press **Q** (or drop the item) so it lands on the ball or right in front of it.
4. You hear a "ding". The bottom of your screen says **you gave it!**

Only your own drop counts for you. A friend cannot give it for you.

## If everybody gave it

The eye closes and sleeps for **10 minutes**. Then it opens and wants something new.

## If someone did NOT give it in time

- The eye turns **red**.
- The ball flies after you. **Walls do not stop it.**
- It goes after the closest player first, then the next one.
- If it touches you, you are caught.
- Holding a **Totem of Undying** saves you... sort of. Watch out!
- When everyone who forgot has been caught once, the eye closes and sleeps for 10 minutes.

## Good to know

- Players in **creative** are just watching. The eye leaves them alone.
- Want harder things to find? An operator types `/given_jar:tier hard`. Easier: `/given_jar:tier easy`.
- An operator can type `/given_jar:skip` to take **10 minutes off** the clock. Careful: if the clock hits zero, the
  hunt starts right away!
- Lost? Type `/given_jar:status` to see the thing, the time and who still has to give it.
- There is only one ball. Making another one does not work.
